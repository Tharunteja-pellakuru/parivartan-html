THE PARIVARTAN APPROACH

Good Interfaces Are Simple. Good Experiences Feel Simple.

UI and UX work together, but they address different problems. User Experience (UX) defines how people navigate, understand and complete tasks within a digital product. User Interface (UI) turns that experience into a clear, consistent and engaging visual interface. At Parivartan, we design both together, so the end result is not merely visually impressive but also user-friendly, practical and ready for development.

User Experience (UX)

Structure & Function

  • User Journeys

    Mapping the way different users browse the product and meet their goals.

  • Information Architecture

    Designing the information, navigation and functionality in such a way that users can quickly find what they need.

  • Wireframes & Prototypes

    Creating and validating the structure and experience before detailed visual design and development begin.

  • Usability & Interaction

    Eliminating unnecessary steps and making complex tasks easier to understand and complete.

User Interface (UI)

Visual & Polish

  • Visual Interface Design

    Designing interfaces that are refined and consistent with both the brand and the purpose of the product.

  • Responsive Design

    Creating experiences that function consistently on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

  • Components & Design Systems

    Creating reusable interface components that maintain a consistent appearance as the product evolves.

  • Developer-Ready Design

    Keeping practicality in mind, we design interfaces with a focus on development, responsiveness and real-world implementation.

OUR METHODOLOGY

From Complexity to Clarity

All projects are different, but our UI/UX method follows a well-defined process, starting with understanding the problem and ending with the delivery of a developable interface.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Requirements & User Research

We start by reviewing the business requirements, users, objectives, existing problems and technical environment. By studying the problem before we design the interface, we can make better decisions at every stage of the project.

02 — STRUCTURE

Architecture & Journeys

We define the user journeys, information architecture, navigation, workflows and content hierarchy. This forms the basis for how users will navigate and interact with the product.

03 — WIREFRAME

Layouts & Functionality

Before any detailed visual design starts, we create wireframes that define screen layouts, interactions and functionality.

04 — PROTOTYPE

Interaction Validation

For products that require interaction validation, we create prototypes to demonstrate how key user journeys, interactions and functionality will work.

05 — DESIGN

Visual Interface & Design System

We create the visual interface, typography, components, layouts, responsive behaviour and design system. The interface balances usability, brand identity and practical implementation.

06 — VALIDATE & REFINE

Review & Refinement

We review the experience against user requirements, business objectives and implementation considerations, and refine it where necessary.

07 — DEVELOPMENT HANDOFF

Specifications & Engineering Collaboration

For development, we prepare designs, components, behaviours and specifications. Our designers and developers collaborate at every stage of the process, reducing the gap between what is designed and what is built.
