Your Website Is Live. But Who Is Looking After It?

By Anand Pohankar, Founder & CEO, Parivartan Software & Multimedia Pvt. Ltd.

Anand Pohankar Founder & CEO
4 min read
Technology & Business Insights

Launching a new website is a major milestone for any business. Once the design is approved, the content is ready, and testing is complete, it goes live. But what about six months or a year later? Many companies stop paying attention to their website after launch. If it loads in a browser and seems to work, most people are satisfied.

Websites need ongoing care. At Parivartan, we have years of experience building and supporting websites for organizations and businesses. We've learned that maintenance is just as important as building the site in the first place.

A Website Is Not a One-Time Investment

A website might look the same to visitors for months, but a lot can change behind the scenes. Hosting environments update, software versions change, new security issues are found, browsers release updates, and third-party services set new requirements. Even sites that aren't updated often can be affected by these changes.

If your business uses a CMS, plugins, payment gateways, enquiry forms, or other integrations, regular technical attention is essential. The real question isn't whether your site will need maintenance, but whether someone is there to handle issues before they become major problems.

What does website maintenance actually involve?

People often think website maintenance just means updating text, uploading images, or adding new pages. While these tasks can be included, technical maintenance goes much further. Depending on your website and the agreement, maintenance might also include:

  • Security and software updates: keeping the platforms, frameworks, plugins and dependencies up to date to address known vulnerabilities.
  • Website backups: maintaining the appropriate backups and ensuring they can be restored when required.
  • Hosting and server monitoring: spotting resource usage issues and technical problems that could impact availability.
  • SSL certificate management: monitoring certificate validity and renewal requirements.
  • Performance and functionality checks: finding broken links, form failures, integration issues and all the other things that are going to impact the user.
  • Content and technical support: dealing with the agreed website updates, troubleshooting and the like through dedicated website maintenance & AMC support.

Different websites need different levels of support. A simple information site will need much less support than a custom application with databases, user accounts, and third-party integrations. Your maintenance plan should match your website's needs.

The Cost of Ignoring Maintenance

A challenge with website maintenance is that its value often comes from preventing problems, not from creating something new. A security update might go unnoticed. You might not need a backup for months. If a technical issue is fixed before it affects visitors, clients may never even know about it.

But if you skip maintenance, you could face many problems. Outdated software can create security risks, broken enquiry forms can cost you business, and expired certificates or hosting issues can make your site unavailable. Fixing these problems later often takes more time and money than regular maintenance would have.

Why an Annual Maintenance Contract Makes Sense

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is a package for ongoing website support. Instead of dealing with separate tickets for every issue, you get a clear set of services and a team familiar with your site's technical setup.

This continuity matters because a team that knows your site's architecture, hosting, integrations, and past development choices can troubleshoot and manage changes more effectively. It also helps your business plan maintenance costs instead of facing unexpected expenses.

An AMC doesn't mean unlimited development support. The agreement should clearly state which services are included, response times, update limits, and what is not covered. Make sure you also understand any third-party costs like hosting, domain renewal, or software licenses.

How We Approach Maintenance at Parivartan

Over the years, we've supported many types of websites for corporate bodies, educational institutions, research organizations, and various businesses with different technical needs. Some sites needed frequent content updates and coordination, while others were more complex due to their applications, hosting, security, and technical support needs.

Working on institutional sites like ARCI and corporate sites like Viyash Scientific has shown us how important continuity is and why understanding the client's environment matters. We view maintenance as an ongoing responsibility, not just something we do when problems arise.

Looking Beyond the Launch

Businesses invest a lot of time and money in planning their websites. They review designs, assess technology, approve content, and make sure the final product reflects their business. It's important to protect that investment after the site goes live.

A website needs to stay functional, secure, relevant, and reliable as both the business and technology change. At Parivartan, building the site through reliable website development is just the start. Helping our clients maintain what we've built is just as important.

Launching a website is a big step, but keeping it running smoothly is an ongoing responsibility.
