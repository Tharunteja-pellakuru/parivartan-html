THE EVOLUTION OF DISCOVERY

Does ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity understand your brand?

FAQS

Questions Businesses Are Asking About Search and AI Visibility

Answers to common questions about traditional search, answer engines, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI search visibility.

Is traditional SEO still important?

Yes. SEO remains an important foundation for digital visibility.

Search engines continue to drive significant discovery, and many of the signals that help search engines understand a website also help other systems understand your business.

What has changed is that SEO is no longer the only form of search visibility businesses should consider.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation.

It focuses on structuring your website and content so search and answer engines can clearly understand your information and identify useful answers to questions users ask.

What is GEO?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimisation.

It focuses on improving how clearly your business, expertise and content can be understood and discovered within AI-powered and generative search environments.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

A simple way to understand it is:

SEO — Be Found.
AEO — Be the Answer.
GEO — Be Part of the AI Conversation.

Together, they form a broader approach to modern search visibility.

Can you help my business appear in ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity?

We can improve the technical, content, entity and authority signals that help AI-powered systems understand your business and monitor how your brand appears across relevant AI search experiences.

However, no agency can legitimately guarantee that a particular AI platform will mention or recommend a business for a specific query.

How do I know whether my company currently appears in AI search results?

We can assess relevant questions and searches across AI-powered platforms to understand whether your brand appears, how it is represented and how its visibility compares with competitors.

Request an AI Visibility Check →

Do I need AEO and GEO if I already invest in SEO?

Increasingly, yes.

SEO provides an important foundation, but people are discovering information in new ways. AEO and GEO extend your visibility strategy beyond traditional search results into answer engines and AI-powered discovery.

Can my existing website be optimised for SEO, AEO and GEO?

Yes.

We can audit the existing website's technical structure, content, information architecture, schema, search visibility and AI-readiness and identify what should be improved.

A complete website rebuild is not always necessary.

How long does SEO, AEO and GEO take?

These are ongoing visibility activities rather than instant advertising channels.

Timelines depend on the current website, competition, existing authority, content, technical condition and the opportunities available.

The objective is to build stronger and more sustainable visibility over time.

How do you measure SEO, AEO and GEO performance?

Measurement can include traditional search rankings, impressions, clicks, organic traffic and conversions alongside emerging indicators such as AI mentions, citations, brand representation and visibility compared with competitors.

As AI search continues to evolve, the methods and tools used to measure this visibility will evolve with it.
