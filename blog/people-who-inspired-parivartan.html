Before There Was Parivartan, There Were People Who Believed in Me

By Anand Pohankar, Founder & CEO, Parivartan Software & Multimedia Pvt. Ltd.

Anand Pohankar Founder & CEO
5 min read
Founder Stories

Looking back at how Parivartan started, I realize that starting a business wasn’t something I ever planned.

In our middle-class family, the goal was to study hard, get a good job, and settle down. Entrepreneurship wasn’t really considered. Still, there were people who saw potential in me before I did.

Without them, Parivartan wouldn’t exist today.

My Father, Santosh Pohankar — The Man Who Believed When Others Didn’t

Starting a business didn’t seem safe, especially since I had no experience or business background. Some people thought I would fail. But my dad supported me.

His support was more than just encouragement. When we started raising funds for Parivartan, he helped however he could. Back then, I had nothing but enthusiasm and a willingness to work, so his belief in me was essential.

My father has passed away, but I often remember how important his belief was. Sometimes, all it takes is one person believing in you to help you get started.

Dr. Hariharan Ramamurthy — The Man Who Saw an Entrepreneur

Interestingly, the idea for Parivartan came from something completely unrelated to business.

I made a multimedia presentation for my sister’s wedding. Dr. Hariharan Ramamurthy saw it during a visit to India from the US. He was impressed and suggested I consider starting a company in web design and multimedia. At the time, I was with friends, but he wanted to talk before returning to the US, so I cut my trip short and came back to Hyderabad.

After that, we had several conversations that eventually led to starting Parivartan. He didn’t just support the idea; he also helped with the initial funding.

I had the technical skills, and he saw the business potential. That moment changed the course of my life. Dr. Hariharan Ramamurthy has passed away, but I’ll never forget his contribution to my journey. He believed in me as an entrepreneur long before I ever thought of it, and I’ll always be grateful for that.

My Mother, Lalit Pohankar — Where the Creativity Began

My mum is an artist, and I think I owe much of my creativity to her.

Growing up around someone so passionate about art and design shaped how I see things. When I got into multimedia, the creative side came naturally. But her influence goes beyond that; the name Parivartan actually came from her. Back in '79 she'd started an initiative whereby people could rent paintings. She called it Parivartan.

Years later, when we needed a name for the company, I chose the same one. It means change, and it felt right.

More than 20 years later, my mother still supports me. She’s always there, still believing in me, through all the changes in the company and everything else.

My Sister, Shruti Pohankar Kulkarni — The Push That Started It All

My sister has always been one of my best critics, and she was the first to get me interested in multimedia. I never imagined that would be my path. I studied multimedia in college, taught at Arena Multimedia at 17, and eventually moved into software development.

Years later, it was a presentation I made for her wedding that caught Dr. Hariharan Ramamurthy’s attention. In many ways, her encouragement is what started everything.

She also gave the company financial support in the early days. It’s amazing how a decision that seems small at the time can end up changing your whole life.

Looking Back

Parivartan began with very limited resources, just a computer at home and plenty of doubt. But we had people who believed in us.

My dad gave me the confidence to try. Dr. Hariharan Ramamurthy saw the opportunity I didn’t. My mum gave me the creative foundation and the name we would use. My sister encouraged me to develop the skills that started it all.

Now that Parivartan has reached 25 years, I find myself thinking about those early days more often. At the start, there were no clients, projects, employees, or achievements—just people who believed I could do it.

Sometimes, your own journey begins because someone else believes in you.

Anand Pohankar
Founder & CEO, Parivartan Software & Multimedia Pvt. Ltd.
