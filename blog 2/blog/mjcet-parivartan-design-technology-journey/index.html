The MJCET Story. Building on Trust, Growing Through Technology

By Anand Pohankar, Founder & CEO, Parivartan Software & Multimedia Pvt. Ltd.

Anand Pohankar Founder & CEO
4 min read
Our Work & Client Stories

Our connection with MJCET, Hyderabad (Muffakham Jah College of Engineering and Technology) began in the earliest days of Parivartan. Instead of starting with a college website or a tech project, our first job was designing a brochure on fly-ash bricks. That assignment marked the beginning of a professional relationship that has grown and evolved over the past 20 years.

Back in 2002, I first met Mr. Ashfaque Jafari through an unusual assignment. We were asked to design a brochure on fly-ash bricks. This project wasn’t related to engineering college websites or digital platforms we would later create. It was simply a design job and a chance to show our abilities.

Mr. Jafari was impressed with our work, and that first project opened the door to something bigger. We were then asked to design the engineering college’s prospectus, a responsibility Parivartan has continued to handle ever since.

Looking back, what makes this partnership special isn’t just how long it’s lasted. It’s how it has naturally grown as both the institution’s needs and our own abilities have changed.

From a Brochure to the College Prospectus

In Parivartan’s early days, design and multimedia were at the core of our work. Each project gave us a chance to shape our approach, understand our clients’ needs, and build confidence in our ability to deliver.

The MJCET prospectus played a key role in that journey. An engineering college prospectus is much more than a collection of photos and course details. It’s the college’s message to prospective students, parents, and anyone wanting to understand the college and its environment.

Over the years, we’ve been responsible for presenting this information through thoughtful design, always aiming to balance the college’s established identity with changing expectations in visual communication.

What I value most is the continuity. Each year, the organization has trusted us with this important communication. For a company that began in 2002, this has played a big role in the relationships we’ve built.

When Design Evolved into Technology

As Parivartan grew, our work changed too. We started mainly with design and multimedia, but over time, we became a technology company building websites, applications, CMSs, and digital platforms.

Our partnership with MJCET followed a similar path. We moved from designing the college prospectus to developing the website, combining our understanding of the college with our growing technical skills.

A college website serves a very different purpose than a typical business site. It needs to meet the needs of many groups—prospective students and parents, current students, staff, departments, and administrators—each looking for something different.

It’s more than just having an attractive interface. The information structure, navigation, content management, and overall user experience must reflect how the institution truly operates.

Knowing MJCET’s identity and communication style gave us a strong foundation as we moved into digital projects.

Understanding the Needs of an Educational Institution

One interesting part of working with schools and colleges is the wide range of information they need to manage and share.

Academics, departments, admissions, staff details, placements, exams, news, and other types of communication all need to be organized so they make sense to different audiences.

During website planning with MJCET, we explored a broader digital platform to support both the college and the Sultan Ul Uloom Education Society. The proposed structure would have included several key areas:

  • Institutional websites: a properly structured website for the engineering college and the educational society.
  • Content management: administrative controls for the management of institutional information and the updating of the website.
  • Department level: dedicated sections for academic departments and their ongoing activities.
  • Faculty and student services: proposed login-based functionality to access and manage relevant information through custom web applications.
  • Digital integrations: consideration of existing institutional systems, authentication, and secure data management requirements.

These discussions showed that educational websites were evolving from simple online brochures into platforms that support communication and the institution’s different workflows.

For us, it became clear that you need to truly understand how an organization works before designing technology for it.

The Value of Relationships That Grow Over Time

It’s very satisfying to work with an organization that has been with us since the very beginning.

In 2002, Parivartan was a young company, building its reputation through design and multimedia. Today, we handle a wide range of work, including website development, custom applications, digital experiences, and ongoing technology support.

MJCET has been central to that growth, from our first brochure to years of prospectus design and later, website development.

Technology has changed, our work has grown a lot, and expectations for digital platforms are very different from 20 years ago. But the basics of a good professional relationship remain the same. You still need to understand the requirements, take ownership of the work, and earn the client’s trust so they want to work with you again.

What the MJCET Journey Means to Us

Our association with MJCET began with that first meeting with Mr. Ashfaque Jafari and the opportunity he offered us. A modest design brief started a relationship that would grow through every stage of Parivartan’s journey.

That’s an important part of our company’s story. It reminds us that not every relationship begins with a big project or major technology work. Sometimes, it starts with something small and the trust you build by doing that small thing well.

Projects will change over time, but it’s the relationships you build through them that truly give everything meaning.

Anand Pohankar
Founder & CEO, Parivartan Software & Multimedia Pvt. Ltd.
