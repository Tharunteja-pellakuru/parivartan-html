PLATFORM EXPERTISE

The Right Platform
for the Business

The platform should fit the requirement, not define it. Different businesses need different approaches to e-commerce. A growing consumer brand may benefit from Shopify and its extensive commerce ecosystem, while a business operating within an existing WordPress environment may find WooCommerce more suitable. Where operational requirements demand something unique, we develop custom commerce platforms.

Shopify

Ideal for growing consumer brands looking for a hosted, managed commerce ecosystem with rapid deployment, secure checkout, global payment rails, and an extensive marketplace of business applications.

  • Hosted infrastructure & managed security
  • Robust checkout & payment gateway support
  • Extensive application ecosystem
  • Fast go-to-market for D2C & retail brands

WooCommerce

Suited for businesses operating within a WordPress environment or requiring greater ownership, customizable databases, bespoke checkout fields, and complete editorial control over content and commerce.

  • Native integration with WordPress CMS
  • 100% control over database & hosting
  • Highly adaptable custom extensions & hooks
  • No recurring platform transaction fees

Custom E-commerce

Engineered for specialized requirements, B2B wholesale portals, multi-tier pricing models, complex subscription logic, and deep bi-directional ERP or warehouse inventory integrations.

  • Custom architectures (Node, React, Python)
  • Tailored B2B workflows, quotes & permissions
  • Direct legacy ERP & proprietary API integration
  • High-volume concurrent transaction handling
MOBILE-FIRST EXPERIENCE

Mobile Commerce
Comes First

For many customers, the entire buying journey happens on a mobile device. Product discovery, comparison, product information, cart management and the checkout process therefore have to function properly on smaller screens. Our e-commerce experiences are designed to be responsive, with attention given to mobile usability at every stage of the customer journey.

FAQS

Questions Businesses Ask Us
About E-commerce Development

Everything you need to know about platforms, custom functionality, integrations, migration and development timelines.

Does Parivartan develop e-commerce websites?

Yes. We design and develop e-commerce websites for businesses that want to sell products online, including new stores, redesigns and redevelopment of existing e-commerce platforms.

Which e-commerce platforms does Parivartan work with?

We work with platforms including Shopify and WooCommerce and can also develop custom e-commerce functionality where the business requirement calls for a more specialised solution.

The platform is recommended after understanding the products, functionality, integrations, expected scale and operational requirements.

Should I use Shopify or WooCommerce for my online store?

Both platforms can be suitable for different requirements.

Shopify provides a managed commerce ecosystem that can work well for businesses looking for a streamlined e-commerce platform. WooCommerce can be appropriate when commerce needs to operate within a WordPress environment or when the project requires greater control over certain aspects of the website.

We recommend the platform after understanding the specific business requirement.

Can you redesign an existing e-commerce website?

Yes. We can review an existing store’s UI/UX, navigation, product structure, mobile experience, technology, performance and search readiness and recommend improvements.

Depending on the requirement, the existing store can be redesigned, reconfigured, migrated or rebuilt.

Can you migrate an existing online store to Shopify or another platform?

Yes, depending on the existing and target platforms.

Migration may include products, categories, customers, content and other relevant data. The exact migration possibilities need to be assessed based on the structure and export capabilities of the existing platform.

Can you integrate payment gateways?

Yes. We can integrate supported payment gateways according to the selected e-commerce platform, business location and payment provider’s technical capabilities (e.g. Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, UPI).

Can you integrate shipping and logistics services?

Yes. Where suitable APIs, applications or platform integrations are available, shipping and logistics services can be connected to the e-commerce platform for live rates, automated labels, and tracking updates.

Can the store be connected to our CRM, ERP or inventory system?

Potentially, yes.

Where appropriate APIs or integration mechanisms are available, the e-commerce platform can exchange information with CRM, ERP, inventory and other business systems. Integration feasibility needs to be evaluated for the specific systems involved.

Will the e-commerce website work properly on mobile devices?

Yes. Responsive and mobile usability are considered throughout the design and development process. The storefront, product discovery, product information, cart and checkout experience are designed with smaller screens in mind.

Will my e-commerce website be SEO-friendly?

Technical SEO considerations such as site architecture, product and category structure, metadata, structured data, performance and mobile responsiveness can be incorporated during development.

Ongoing SEO, AEO and GEO are separate activities that can continue after the store is launched.

Can my team manage products after the website is launched?

Yes. Depending on the selected platform and user permissions, authorised team members can manage products, categories, pricing, inventory, images, content, offers and other relevant store information through an intuitive administrative dashboard.

Can you add new functionality to an existing Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Yes, depending on the requirement and the technical capabilities of the existing platform. We first assess the current store, theme, applications, plugins and integrations before recommending how the required functionality should be implemented.

How long does it take to develop an e-commerce website?

The timeline depends on the number of products, catalogue complexity, UI/UX requirements, platform, custom functionality, integrations and content readiness.

After understanding the requirement, we can define the project scope and estimated development timeline.

Do you provide support after the e-commerce website is launched?

Yes. Depending on the engagement, Parivartan can provide ongoing technical maintenance, platform support, enhancements, integrations and continued development after launch.

Does Parivartan also manage digital marketing for e-commerce brands?

Yes. Social media management, creative campaigns, content and performance marketing are handled through Swipe Social, our specialist creative and digital marketing brand.

Parivartan focuses on the technology behind the e-commerce platform, while Swipe Social can support the marketing and customer acquisition side of the business.
