Parivartan Excellence Awards

Celebrating the People Who Make the Difference.

Great work is rarely the result of one person or one moment.

It comes from people who consistently show up, take responsibility, solve problems, support others, and contribute beyond their job descriptions. The Parivartan Excellence Awards were created to recognise those people.

5+ Years Running
60+ Honorees
6 Categories
CONTRIBUTION FIRST

Recognition That Comes
From Within

We introduced the Parivartan Excellence Awards in 2023 to recognise the people whose contribution helps Parivartan move forward. Performance matters. But contribution is about more than performance alone.

It can be about taking ownership, helping a colleague, solving a difficult problem, learning something new, stepping up when needed, or simply being someone the team can depend on.

The awards acknowledge those contributions and celebrate the people behind them.

BEHIND THE TROPHY

Every Award Has a Story Behind It.

The trophy or certificate captures a moment. What matters more is what happened before it.

The work. The effort. The learning. The responsibility. The teamwork. And sometimes, the willingness to do just a little more than what was expected.

The Parivartan Excellence Awards are our way of saying that those things matter. And so do the people who make them happen.
