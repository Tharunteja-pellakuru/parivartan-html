MOBILE APP

Tandoori Flames

mobile app UI/UX food ordering

OVERVIEW

Designed and developed a feature-rich mobile app for Tandoori Flames to provide customers with an effortless online food ordering, menu browsing, table reservation, and real-time order tracking experience.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

  • Interactive digital menu with rich food categories, customization options, and special chef recommendations
  • Seamless online food ordering system with instant cart management and flexible payment integration
  • Real-time order status tracking from kitchen preparation to doorstep delivery or pickup
  • Personalized user profile for managing saved delivery addresses, favorite dishes, and order history
