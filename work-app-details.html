Rental Management Application

OVERVIEW

Developed a centralized rental management application to efficiently manage properties, tenants, locations, and rental payments. The platform streamlines tenant onboarding, communication, payment tracking, and daily property operations through a scalable and user-friendly system.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

  • Developed a centralized rental management platform for handling properties, tenants, and locations efficiently
  • Enabled seamless tenant management with streamlined onboarding, tracking, and communication features

  • Built a scalable and user-friendly system to simplify day-to-day property rental operations and management
