FAQs

Questions Businesses Ask Us About UI/UX Design

Answers to common questions about our UI/UX design process, deliverables, tools, design systems, and developer collaboration.

What is UI/UX design?

UI/UX design covers both how a digital product works and how users interact with it. UX focuses on structure, journeys, usability and interactions, while UI focuses on the visual interface, components, layouts and presentation.

Does Parivartan provide UI/UX design for existing websites and applications?

Yes. We can evaluate an existing website or application and redesign its user experience and interface without necessarily rebuilding the entire product.

Where technology limitations affect the experience, we can also recommend redevelopment or modernisation.

Do you design UI/UX for enterprise applications and dashboards?

Yes. We design interfaces for custom applications, enterprise portals, dashboards, workflows, reporting systems and other business platforms involving complex processes or multiple user roles.

Do you provide UI/UX design for mobile apps?

Yes. We design mobile application experiences for iOS, Android and cross-platform applications, including user journeys, wireframes, interfaces, components and interactive prototypes where required.

Can you design the UI/UX while another company develops the application?

Yes. UI/UX can be undertaken as an independent engagement.

We can provide structured designs, components, interaction guidance and developer-ready assets for implementation by your internal or external development team.

What is the difference between website design and UI/UX design?

Website design typically focuses on creating the structure and visual presentation of a website.

UI/UX design goes deeper into how users navigate, interact with information, complete tasks and experience the overall digital product.

Do you create wireframes and prototypes before designing?

Yes, where the project requires them.

For applications and complex platforms, wireframes and prototypes can help establish workflows, structure and interactions before detailed visual design begins.

Can you create a design system for an existing product?

Yes. We can develop or standardise a design system for an existing website, application or digital product to improve consistency and make future development easier to manage.

Do you conduct UX audits of existing websites and applications?

Yes. We can review an existing digital product for navigation, information architecture, usability, interface consistency, responsiveness and other experience issues and recommend improvements.

Does Parivartan also develop the products it designs?

Yes. Parivartan provides website development, custom application development and mobile app development in addition to UI/UX design.

Clients can engage us for UI/UX alone or for the complete design-and-development lifecycle.
