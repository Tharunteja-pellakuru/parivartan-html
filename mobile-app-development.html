WHAT WE BUILD

Mobile Applications Built
for a Purpose

A successful mobile application needs more than a good-looking interface.

It must be built around what users want to achieve, simplify key actions and maintain a reliable connection with the technology and information behind it.

We design and build mobile apps for different business needs.

01

Customer-Facing Apps

Mobile applications that enable customers to access services, manage accounts, view information, place orders and interact directly with your business.

02

Business & Enterprise Apps

Applications for mobile use which enable employees and teams to obtain information, carry out tasks and interact with business systems when working from various locations.

03

Service Applications

Applications that are designed with specific services, bookings, requests, subscriptions, transactions or other customer interactions in mind.

04

Companion Apps

Mobile applications that work alongside an existing website, platform, product or business application.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Native or Cross-Platform? The
Requirement Comes First.

The right development approach depends on what the application needs to do, who will use it, the devices it needs to support, integrations, performance requirements and the long-term product roadmap.

Depending on the requirement, we can develop:

FAQs

Answers to Common Questions
on Mobile App Development

Have specific questions about native versus cross-platform apps, App Store guidelines, or development timelines? Find straightforward answers below or reach out directly to our team.

Does Parivartan develop both Android and iOS applications?

Yes. We develop mobile applications for Android and iOS, including cross-platform applications where appropriate to the project requirements.

What types of mobile applications does Parivartan develop?

We develop a wide range of mobile applications including enterprise apps, customer-facing portals, on-demand services, eCommerce solutions, and internal operations tools tailored to your business needs.

Should I build a native app or a cross-platform app?

The choice depends on your feature requirements, budget, timeline, and performance expectations. We analyze your requirements to recommend either native (Swift/Kotlin) or cross-platform (Flutter) development.

Does Parivartan develop apps using Flutter?

Yes, we have deep expertise in building scalable cross-platform applications using Flutter, delivering near-native performance with a single unified codebase for iOS and Android.

Can you design the UI/UX for my mobile application?

Yes. Our design team creates comprehensive mobile UI/UX designs, from wireframing and user journey mapping to high-fidelity prototypes and design systems tailored for touch interactions.

Can a mobile app connect to our existing website or business application?

Yes. We build robust API integrations that connect your mobile application directly with your existing website, database, CRM, ERP, and third-party systems in real time.

Can you develop the backend for a mobile application?

Yes, we architect and develop scalable, secure cloud backends, databases, microservices, and REST/GraphQL APIs that power all mobile features and workflows.

Can you integrate payment gateways into mobile apps?

Yes. We integrate industry-standard payment processors including Stripe, Razorpay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, in-app purchases, and mobile wallets with full PCI-DSS compliance.

Can you add push notifications to an application?

Yes, we configure targeted push notification systems using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Apple Push Notification service (APNs) with segmentation and automated triggers.

Can you take over an existing mobile application?

Yes. We can audit, refactor, upgrade, and maintain existing codebases, resolving performance bottlenecks, modernizing dependencies, and adding new features.

How long does it take to develop a mobile application?

Timeline depends on scope and complexity. A standard MVP typically takes 8 to 12 weeks, while larger enterprise applications with complex integrations can take 3 to 6 months.

Do you help with publishing the application to app stores?

Yes. We manage the entire submission process for Apple App Store and Google Play Store, ensuring compliance with review guidelines, asset preparation, and launch readiness.

Do you provide support after the app is launched?

Yes. We provide ongoing support, monitoring, SLA-backed maintenance, OS version compatibility updates, and feature enhancements post-launch.
