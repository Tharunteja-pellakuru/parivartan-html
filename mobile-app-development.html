HOW WE WORK

A Proven Lifecycle from Concept to App Store Launch

Transparent, milestone-driven execution ensuring App Store approval and high user ratings.

Building a world-class mobile app requires rigorous engineering, iterative test builds, and deep platform compliance. Our 8-stage methodology eliminates surprises and accelerates release.

01

Discovery & Strategy

Target audience definition, feature scoping, monetization strategy, and platform recommendation (Native vs. Cross-Platform).

02

Mobile UX & Wireframing

User journeys, gesture patterns, navigation flows, and interactive low-fidelity prototypes designed for thumb reach and ergonomics.

03

UI Design & Prototypes

High-fidelity UI mockups complying with Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Google Material 3 design systems with dark mode support.

04

Agile Frontend Development

Sprint-based modular coding using Swift, Kotlin, or Flutter with continuous integration builds delivered weekly via TestFlight and Play Console.

05

Backend & API Integration

Connecting authentication services, payment processors, push notification relays, third-party APIs, and offline data synchronization.

06

Device & QA Testing

Exhaustive testing across real iOS and Android devices checking battery drain, memory leaks, slow network states, and automated regressions.

07

App Store & Play Store Deployment

App Store Optimization (ASO), metadata preparation, privacy disclosures, and end-to-end guidance through Apple and Google review approvals.

08

Monitoring & OS Updates

Post-launch crash monitoring, performance analytics, user rating tracking, and timely annual updates for major new iOS and Android versions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions on Mobile App Development

Everything you need to know about platforms, approvals, and budgets.

Have specific questions about native versus cross-platform apps, App Store guidelines, or development timelines? Find straightforward answers below or reach out directly to our team.

Which is better for my project: Native (iOS & Android) or Cross-Platform (Flutter & React Native)?

Both approaches have distinct strengths depending on your product goals.

Native (Swift & Kotlin) is ideal for apps requiring heavy hardware acceleration, AR/VR, complex background tasks, or bleeding-edge OS APIs.

Cross-Platform (Flutter or React Native) allows you to build a single unified codebase that runs seamlessly across both iOS and Android, reducing time-to-market and development costs by up to 40% while delivering near-native performance for 95% of business applications.

How long does it take to develop and launch a mobile application?

Development timelines depend on feature complexity and scope:

• MVP / Fast-Track Application: 8 to 12 weeks.
• Medium-Scale Business Application: 3 to 5 months.
• Complex Enterprise / Real-time Ecosystem: 6+ months with continuous milestone iterations.

We provide transparent milestone schedules and weekly demo builds throughout the engineering cycle.

Do you handle the App Store and Google Play Store submission and approval process?

Yes, completely. We manage the entire store deployment process, including Developer Account configuration, code signing, SSL certificate management, privacy policy compliance, App Store Review Guidelines adherence, screenshot generation, and App Store Optimization (ASO).

Can you integrate our existing web platform, database, or ERP into the mobile app?

Yes. We regularly connect mobile frontends to existing web applications, databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB), ERPs (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), payment gateways, and proprietary internal systems via RESTful APIs and GraphQL.

How do you ensure data security and user privacy in mobile applications?

We build apps following zero-trust principles. We implement end-to-end data encryption (AES-256), SSL/TLS pinning, secure storage (iOS Keychain and Android Keystore), biometric authentication (Face ID & Fingerprint), and full compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, and industry-specific privacy mandates.

Can the mobile app work offline without an active internet connection?

Yes. We architect offline-first mobile applications using local embedded databases (SQLite, Realm, or WatermelonDB). Users can browse content, draft forms, or perform tasks without internet, and the application seamlessly synchronizes all cached modifications once network connection is restored.

What happens when Apple or Google release new iOS and Android OS updates?

Our continuous maintenance contracts ensure that as Apple and Google unveil new annual operating system versions (such as iOS 18/19 and Android 15/16), we test and update your codebase against developer betas, ensuring zero day compatibility, avoiding deprecation issues, and taking advantage of new OS capabilities.

Can you redesign or take over an existing mobile app developed by another agency?

Yes. We perform a structured technical and UI/UX audit of legacy mobile applications. We can refactor and stabilize existing codebases, modernize outdated UI, eliminate performance bottlenecks, or perform an incremental migration to modern frameworks.

How do you test the app across different smartphones, screen sizes, and OS versions?

We test apps using both an in-house lab of physical smartphones/tablets and cloud-based automated device farms (BrowserStack / Firebase Test Lab). We test across multiple screen densities, aspect ratios, OS versions, and unstable network conditions to ensure consistent performance.

Do you assist with in-app payments, subscriptions, and wallet integrations?

Yes. We implement Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2), Google Play Billing, Stripe, Razorpay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, complete with server-side receipt validation, auto-renewing subscription logic, and entitlement management (e.g. RevenueCat).

How do push notifications and user engagement tracking work?

We integrate push notification services (Apple APNs, Firebase Cloud Messaging, OneSignal) supporting rich media notifications, deep linking directly to specific app screens, automated segmented campaigns, and behavioral analytics (Mixpanel, Google Analytics for Firebase).

Who owns the source code and intellectual property once the app is developed?

You own 100% of the intellectual property and code. Upon project completion and milestone settlement, all Git repositories, design files (Figma), database schemas, and App Store developer credentials are fully transferred to your team.
